# ARMADA

## Reliable Conversational Domain-Specific Data Exploration and Analysis

ARMADA is a foundational framework that bridges formal data retrieval and
natural language innovation for reliable assistive technology.

Conversational AI and large language models (LLMs) can answer complex
questions through simple conversations. However, their reasoning is often
inscrutable and they can be affected by bias, hallucinations, and high
computational costs. ARMADA addresses these challenges in sensitive domains
by combining LLMs with structured data management.

The ARMADA doctoral network trains **15 Early Stage Researchers** in
Conversational AI. Researchers develop expertise in natural language
processing, machine learning, data management, and algorithms to evaluate and
improve the reliability of LLMs. The network brings together **8
organizations from 7 countries** in an interdisciplinary scheme for sharing
knowledge and skills.

The project focuses on:

- alignment with domain knowledge;
- explainability and soundness of answers;
- interactive correction workflows;
- effective and efficient computation; and
- training through schools, workshops, and courses in scientific writing,
  innovation, supervision, and management.

Reliable LLMs should produce timely, consistent, and verifiable answers that
help users make important decisions in areas such as medicine, education, and
business intelligence. ARMADA also contributes technical expertise to the
responsible adoption and regulation of AI in Europe.

## Key innovations

- **Conversational Data Analytics:** Interact with data using natural
  language while combining LLMs with structured query languages.
- **Reliability first:** Put efficiency, grounding, explainability, soundness,
  and guidance at the center of conversational data analytics.
- **Compositional framework:** Integrate LLMs with robust data management
  techniques to address hallucinations and opaque reasoning.
- **Beyond generative:** Build systems that are domain-aware, verifiable, and
  able to justify their responses.
- **Future vision:** Develop architectures, metrics, and methodologies for
  reliable, explainable, and efficient AI-driven analytics.
